Object Manipulation

The game

  • You will be presented with a rotatable object in a room

Controls

  • ← → : rotate the object using the arrow keys
  • ␣ : press the spacebar (or tap the screen) to interact with the object

General rules

  • The textures take a moment to download. After that, a countdown from 3 starts before the game begins
  • Do not exit fullscreen during the game
  • Play on your main monitor — not on an external / attached display
  • Close all other apps and browser tabs
  • The session lasts at most 20 minutes (it may end sooner)
  • The game can be solved fully in less than 20 minutes. Think about a strategy
  • Between levels the screen goes black and a clock at the top shows the time left in the session
  • Your data is saved automatically after every trial

Hint: pay attention to the object's details

Heart rate band (optional)

You can record your heart rate during the game. Supported devices: the Polar H9 and H10 chest bands and the Polar Verity Sense. This step is optional.

  • Chest band: moisten the two electrode areas on the inside of the strap with water
  • Fasten the strap around your chest, just below the chest muscles, and snap the sensor onto it (Verity Sense: wear it on your forearm or upper arm, sensor against the skin)
  • Close any other app that is connected to the band (for example on your phone)
  • Polar H10: switch it on and pair it in your operating system's Bluetooth settings first. Then connect and synchronize it here within 50 seconds
  • Press “Connect band” and choose your device (“Polar H9 …”, “Polar H10 …” or “Polar Sense …”) in the browser window
  • Wait until the heart rate appears below (H10 and Verity Sense: then synchronize as shown below), then go back and continue
