Polar H10 + Verity Sense: clock sync test

Streams both sensors over Web Bluetooth and measures each sensor's clock against this laptop's from spacebar presses and knocks. Nothing leaves this page: use “Download session” at the bottom.

Session sync

At the start and at the end of a session, press the spacebar 10 times, firmly, about once a second, with a sensor that feels the presses: the H10 pod held between your fingers (keep touching both electrodes, or clip it back within 30 s) or the forearm wearing the Verity Sense. Every connected sensor that feels the presses is synced; offset from the syncs, drift from the line through them.

Blocks

Every sync and test block. Extra tests: a press block between the syncs is a held-out check (its offset should match the line through the syncs); a knock block (knock the H10 pod with the fist of the arm wearing the Verity Sense, 5 times, then Enter) syncs one sensor through the other.

Selected block (click a row): each line is one press or knock, scaled to its peak, on the laptop clock; dots are the detected onsets. Presses are aligned on the keydown (0), knocks on the reference sensor's impact.

Arrival delay

Each frame's arrival time minus its device time, minus the envelope line (ms). The bottom edge is the line; the spread above it is the wait for a radio slot. A thin flat band means every frame waits the same time (“locked”): the arrivals then hide the drift, and it must come from two press blocks.
